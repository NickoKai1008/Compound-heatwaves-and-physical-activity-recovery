# Confidential Figshare review record

Review link: [https://figshare.com/s/2f704478ce1f00db0ac0](https://figshare.com/s/2f704478ce1f00db0ac0)

This confidential record supports anonymous review of the spatial data pipeline for the 75-city study.

## Deposited contents

- `meta_variables/`: eight built-environment and socioeconomic moderators; harmonized 10-km GeoPackage; raw and standardized Mean/Gini matrices; source layers; field definitions; provenance records; correlation matrices; VIF diagnostics; and diagnostic maps.
- `physical_activity_sample/`: a de-identified Austin analytical example linking physical-activity observations to the study grid, including a grid-level activity matrix and an origin-to-grid audit sample.
- `heatwave_sample/`: Austin grid-level temperature inputs used to construct the local heatwave exposure series.
- `city_center_coordinates_75cities/`: the 75-city coordinate table and city-boundary shapefile used to define spatial extent and city-level assignments.
- `FIGSHARE_ANONYMOUS_REVIEW_DESCRIPTION.md`: contents, intended use and access conditions.

## Supported review tasks

The record supports reconstruction of the Mean/Gini spatial predictor matrices, assessment of spatial coverage and multicollinearity, inspection of the Austin origin-to-grid linkage and verification of the matching between physical-activity and heatwave inputs.

## Data access

The GitHub compact tables support registered figure redraws. National record-level physical-activity data are managed under the provider's access and reuse terms. The Austin review sample is de-identified and retains the spatial and temporal fields required for auditing the linkage workflow. A public record and persistent citation will follow the journal decision and applicable sharing permissions.
